# Game ideas on the shelf

Not built yet. Each one would be a new file in `js/games/` plus a card on the
home page, and would feed the same scoreboard as the other five.

## Memory Match
- **Format:** teams take turns; whole room watches.
- **How it plays:** a grid of face-down Christmas cards (start with 16, 8 pairs).
  A team flips two. A match stays face up, scores, and earns another flip; a miss
  flips back and the turn passes.
- **Points:** 100 per pair.
- **Needs:** card artwork (8–12 simple icons), grid size option for a shorter or longer game.

## Christmas Charades
- **Format:** one student acts, their team guesses.
- **How it plays:** the screen shows a prompt only the actor should see (so the
  actor stands at the laptop, or the projector is blanked and the prompt shows on
  the laptop only), with a 60-second timer. Host taps "Got it" or "Pass" and the
  next prompt comes up. Teams rotate.
- **Points:** 100 per prompt guessed in the minute.
- **Needs:** a prompt list (decorating a tree, wrapping a present, shepherd
  watching sheep, building a snowman, and so on), and a decision on how to hide
  the prompt from the room.

## This or That
- **Format:** whole room, on their feet. No scoring; an icebreaker.
- **How it plays:** two options fill the two halves of the screen ("real tree or
  fake tree," "open one gift on Christmas Eve or wait"). Students walk to the
  side of the room that matches. Host taps for the next one.
- **Points:** none.
- **Needs:** a list of 20–30 pairs. Easiest of the five to build.

## Pin the Nose
- **Format:** one player per team at the laptop.
- **How it plays:** a reindeer appears somewhere on the screen for two seconds,
  then vanishes. The player clicks where the nose was. Closest click wins the
  round; the reindeer gets smaller and faster each round.
- **Points:** 100 for every team you beat, per round (same as Reindeer Dash).
- **Needs:** nothing new; reuses the reindeer art.

## Nativity Order
- **Format:** every team plays the same puzzle, one team at a time, against a clock.
- **How it plays:** six to eight events from Luke 1–2 and Matthew 1–2 appear
  shuffled (Gabriel sent to Mary, the decree from Caesar Augustus, the birth, the
  angel and the shepherds, Simeon in the temple, the wise men, the flight to
  Egypt). The team drags them into order. Fastest correct order wins.
- **Points:** 100 for every team you beat.
- **Needs:** the event list with references, and a decision on how to order the
  events that Luke and Matthew don't sequence against each other (for example,
  the temple visit in Luke 2:22–38 and the wise men in Matthew 2). Safest is to
  keep each puzzle within one Gospel.
